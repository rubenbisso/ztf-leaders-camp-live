Debts

If you are indebted:

  1. Make a list of all your debts and the people you owe.
  2. Pray, asking the Lord to convict you of the sin of debt and to give you His hatred of debt.
  3. Repent of having disobeyed the Scriptures by going into debt.
  4. Pray, asking the Lord to give you what it takes to pay back each of your debts.
  5. Make a plan for how you will pay back your debts.
  6. As soon as you have money, begin paying back your debts one after another.
Fasting

We encourage you to fast three complete days every month for your spiritual revival, fruitfulness and personal needs.

During your three-day fast, please carry the following burdens:

  1. First day: your personal spiritual revival (Pray to better understand and obey the Word and the words of God, praying especially for obedience to the Bertoua message).
  2. Second day: bearing fruit (Pray to win at least one soul who remains, to receive spiritual gifts and for your character).
  3. Third day: your personal needs (make a list of all your needs and pray each day for some of the needs on your list).
